How ShuttleDraw works

A lightweight badminton doubles tournament manager for 2 groups, 10 pairs and 3 courts. Everything runs in the browser — no server, no accounts.

Group stage

  • Round-robin inside each group — every pair plays every other pair once
  • 5 pairs per group → 10 matches per group → 20 group matches
  • Single game to 21. Win = 2 points, loss = 0. No draws.
  • Ranked by points, then point difference, then points scored

Rolling court scheduling

  • No fixed time slots — a court picks up the next eligible match the moment it frees up
  • Court 1 runs 6:00 AM–9:00 AM, Courts 2 & 3 run 6:00 AM–8:00 AM
  • Teams waiting longest are prioritised; back-to-back matches are avoided where possible
  • A team can never be on two courts at once

Knockout

  • Top 4 from each group qualify automatically
  • QF (sets to 11) → SF (sets to 15) → Final (sets to 21), all best of 3

Data

  • Saved to browser localStorage — refresh-safe
  • Export a JSON backup any time from Settings; import it on another device
