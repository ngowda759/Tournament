How ShuttleDraw works

A round-robin badminton tournament manager. Every team plays every other team exactly once.

Two entry modes

  • Enter teams directly — name each team manually with an optional description
  • Pair players — enter boys and girls separately, the system randomly pairs them into mixed doubles teams. Hit Reshuffle to try different pairings.

Scoring rules

  • Win = 3 points
  • Loss = 0 points · No draws
  • Tiebreaker: most wins

Data persistence

  • All data saved to browser localStorage
  • Closing and reopening the tab restores your tournament
  • Data never leaves your device

Reset tournament?

This will permanently clear all teams, matches, and results. This cannot be undone.
